Show drawings automatically when I come back

By default Handmark only touches a page after you click its icon or press the shortcut, and your drawings come back when you do. Turn this on to see them as soon as a page opens. Chrome will ask to let Handmark “read and change data on all websites”: Handmark only uses this to draw its own layer and never reads, changes or sends page content.

Saved drawings

Privacy

  • Everything stays on this computer. Drawings are kept in Chrome’s extension storage. Handmark has no server, no account, no analytics and makes no network requests.
  • Incognito windows are never saved. If you allow Handmark in incognito, drawings there disappear when you close the tab.
  • Screenshots and recordings are kept on this computer only: for up to a day in Handmark’s own storage so the editor can open them, and in your Downloads/Handmark folder when you save them.
  • Backups are plain files you control. They contain the addresses and titles of pages you drew on.
