# Handmark privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Handmark is a browser extension for drawing on web pages. This policy explains what it stores and what it doesn't.

## What Handmark collects
**Nothing.** Handmark has no server, no account, no analytics and no advertising. It makes no network requests and sends no data to the developer or anyone else.

## What Handmark stores on your computer
Using Chrome's extension storage, which stays on your device:
- **Your drawings** for each page you draw on: the marks, any note text you type, and the window width at the time.
- **The address and title** of each of those pages, so drawings can come back when you return and so you can find them in the saved pages list.
- **Your settings**: tool, colour, size, layout and similar.

Handmark does not read, store or send the content of the pages you visit, your browsing history, form entries, passwords or cookies.

## Incognito
If you allow Handmark in incognito windows, nothing you draw there is stored.

## Screenshots, recordings and backups
- Screenshots and recordings are kept in Handmark's own local storage for up to a day so the editor can open them, and saved to your Downloads folder (`Downloads/Handmark`) when you choose. They never leave your computer.
- Recording uses Chrome's tab capture only for the tab and area you choose, only while the recording runs.
- Backup files are created only when you click **Export**. They contain your drawings, page addresses and titles, and are saved where you choose.

Handmark never uploads either.

## Permissions
- **Active tab and scripting:** to draw on the tab where you start Handmark.
- **Storage:** to keep your drawings and settings.
- **Side panel:** to show the controls.
- **Downloads:** to save screenshots.
- **Access to all sites (optional, off by default):** only if you turn on "Show drawings automatically", so your saved drawings appear when a page opens. Handmark uses it only to draw its own layer.

## Deleting your data
- Delete one page's drawings from the side panel or settings page.
- Delete everything with **Settings → Delete all drawings**.
- Uninstalling the extension removes all of its stored data.
